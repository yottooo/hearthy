# Emberlight Studio: Team Handbook

## Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Sofia time, when everyone should be reachable. Outside core hours,
plan your day as you like.

Everyone can work remotely up to **3 days a week**. Tuesdays are office days for the whole studio,
because that is when planning and design reviews happen. Fully remote arrangements need approval
from your lead and are reviewed every six months.

## Time off

- 25 days of paid holiday per year, plus public holidays.
- Up to 5 unused days roll over to the next year and expire on 31 March.
- Book anything longer than 3 days at least 2 weeks in advance in the Leave channel.
- Sick days don't need a doctor's note for the first 2 days.

## On-call

Engineers rotate on-call weekly, handing over on Monday at 11:00.

If production is down between 19:00 and 08:00, page the on-call engineer through the Pager app. If
they don't acknowledge within 15 minutes, the page escalates to the Head of Engineering, Mira
Petrova. On-call weeks are paid an extra 150 EUR, plus time off in lieu for any night-time incident
lasting more than an hour.

## Equipment

New starters get a laptop of their choice (up to 2,500 EUR) and a 600 EUR home-office budget that
renews every two years. Keep receipts and submit them through Expenses within 30 days.

# Ashford K2 Smart Kettle: User Manual

## Overview

The Ashford K2 is a 1.7 litre variable-temperature kettle with five presets: 40°C (baby formula),
70°C (green tea), 80°C (white tea), 90°C (oolong) and 100°C (black tea and coffee). The keep-warm
function holds the selected temperature for up to 45 minutes.

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning                                  | What to do                                                   |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| E1   | Temperature sensor fault                 | Unplug for 10 minutes. If it persists, contact support.      |
| E2   | Kettle lifted off the base while heating | Place it back on the base and press Start again.             |
| E3   | Scale build-up detected                  | Run the descaling cycle (see below).                         |
| E4   | Dry-boil protection triggered            | The kettle is empty. Let it cool for 5 minutes, then refill. |
| E5   | Firmware update failed                   | Hold Start and Keep Warm together for 8 seconds to retry.    |

## Descaling

Descale every 6 weeks in hard-water areas, or whenever E3 appears.

1. Fill to the MAX line with a 1:1 mix of water and white vinegar.
2. Hold the 90°C button for 3 seconds until the light blinks orange.
3. The kettle heats, rests for 20 minutes and beeps three times when finished.
4. Empty it and boil fresh water twice before using it again.

## Warranty

The K2 has a 3-year warranty covering the heating element and electronics. The glass body is covered
for 1 year. Register the kettle within 30 days of purchase to extend the warranty by 12 months.
